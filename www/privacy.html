EFI Calculator — Privacy Policy

The EFI Calculator (the website and the iOS app) does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies, and no third-party services.

What happens to what you enter

Everything you enter into the worksheet is processed entirely on your device, in the page itself. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere; closing or reloading the page clears it.

Network access

The iOS app ships all of its content inside the app bundle and works fully offline. The website uses your browser's standard caching so it also works offline; no data leaves your device in either case.
